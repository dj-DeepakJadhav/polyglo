Did the recordings say the right words?

Every recording is transcribed back and compared to the script it was read from. WER is the share of words that came back different — 0.00 is a word-perfect match, 0.30 means roughly a third were wrong. Below 0.10 passes; higher is retried on another voice, and anything that never clears is held back for a human. This counts every attempt, not just the final one.
